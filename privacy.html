Privacy policy · 3 October 2026

Your day. Your data.

ChinOS is a personal dashboard and assistant operated by Isaac Chin for his own use. This policy explains how the private app handles connected information. This public website contains information about the app and does not provide access to the dashboard.

Information the app accesses

With the account owner’s permission, ChinOS reads Google Calendar calendars and events to display an agenda and answer scheduling questions. It can create, update or delete calendar events when requested, including invitations to people the owner names. It reads the owner’s Google Contacts and “Other contacts”, read-only, to find email addresses for those invitations. It also reads the Google Sheets selected by the owner for a personal tracker. The app connects separately to Notion and GitHub for tasks and project information. The Notion workspace also holds a contacts table the owner maintains (names, how each person relates to him, when he last saw them, and notes), which the app reads to prepare check-ins.

ChinOS processes typed, spoken or Telegram requests (including photos and forwarded messages the owner sends), assistant replies, contacts, profile notes and explicitly saved memories. It stores conversation history, usage records, action records and the OAuth credentials needed to maintain authorized connections.

How information is used and processed

Connected data is used to show the dashboard, answer the owner’s questions and perform requested actions. When the owner has switched it on, the assistant can also start a short check-in on its own in the evening, about a birthday, an anniversary or someone the owner has not seen in a while. To write it, the app uses the owner’s calendar events and free time and the contacts table described above. A check-in may suggest a plan or propose a calendar event or task, which is added only after the owner confirms it, and it never invites anyone. Relevant dashboard information and requests are sent to Anthropic to generate assistant responses; for a check-in this includes the names, relationships and shared activities of the people involved. Voice requests are transcribed on the app’s server where possible; otherwise the audio is sent to a transcription provider through OpenRouter. Speech playback is generated in the owner’s browser. Telegram carries the owner’s chats with the assistant, including its replies and any check-ins, and operational alerts.

The private app and its persistent data are hosted on Railway. Tailscale controls access to the dashboard. Google and the other connected providers process requests to their own services under their respective policies. ChinOS does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Application data and connection credentials are stored in private server storage. The owner keeps private backups on Railway, on a Mac and in a private GitHub repository. Exported backups omit OAuth credentials and pending actions. Conversation history and saved memories remain until the owner removes them. Deleting live data does not immediately remove copies from existing backups.

Control and deletion

The owner can disconnect Google through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically erase information already saved in conversation history or backups. The owner can remove saved data and backups directly.

This public website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and do not use analytics, cookies or a sign-in form. GitHub may process ordinary network and access-log information to deliver them.

Changes

ChinOS has no users besides its owner, so there is no public support channel. Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised date.
