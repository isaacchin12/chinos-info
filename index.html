A personal dashboard & assistant

One place for the day ahead.

ChinOS brings a personal calendar, tasks and project updates into a private dashboard. Its assistant, Summer, answers questions about the day and helps with requested calendar and task changes.

Why Google access is requested

Google Calendar access lets ChinOS display upcoming events, answer scheduling questions and make calendar changes requested by the account owner. Read-only Google Contacts access lets it find the email addresses of people the owner asks to invite. A separately configured Google Sheets connection supplies the owner’s personal tracker.

A private, personal app

ChinOS is operated by Isaac Chin for personal use. It is not open for public account registration and has no users besides its owner. This site provides information about the app; the dashboard has separate private access controls.

Read the privacy policy for information about data access, processing, storage and deletion, and the terms of use.
